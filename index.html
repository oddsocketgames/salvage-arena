First launch: "Windows protected your PC"

The launcher isn't code-signed yet (signing certificates cost money and this is a small playtest), so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. When you run it the first time:

  1. Your browser may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Choose Keep.
  2. Run SalvageArena-Launcher.exe. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The launcher installs the game, updates it, and starts it. You only see the warning once.

Only download the launcher from this page or the Odd Socket GitHub releases. If you got it anywhere else, don't run it.

What's in it

Pick one of nine salvage robots, each with its own gun, ultimate and quirks, and fight through 15 waves across the Scrapyard, the Toxic Dump and the Foundry. Between waves, spend scrap in the Salvage Yard: modules that link up on your chassis, drones that fight beside you, gun upgrades, and relics that change the rules of your run.

  • 9 robots, 9 signature guns with four upgrade tiers each, and 9 ultimates
  • 48 relics and 25 relic synergies; 59 modules that link on a chassis grid
  • 4 arenas with their own hazards and 6 bosses
  • Online co-op for up to 4: revive your friends, ping targets, shared DPS meter
  • Endless mode, daily runs, weekly challenges, boss rush and 8 threat levels
  • Save & quit mid-run, full controller support (or mouse and keyboard)

Play with friends

  1. Open CO-OP → FRIENDS in the game. You get your own friend code, like AB3D-EF7H.
  2. Send your code to a friend (or add theirs). Once they accept, you can see when each other is online.
  3. INVITE a friend (it hosts a lobby for you) and they get a pop-up to accept, or JOIN a friend who's hosting. Up to 4 players, and friends can join a run that's already going.

Everyone needs the same version. Starting the game through the launcher keeps you up to date, so keep the launcher somewhere handy (your Desktop is fine) and use it to play.

Good to know

  • Found a bug? Pause (Esc, or START on a controller) and choose REPORT A BUG. It saves a text file and a screenshot; send them to us however you like. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.
  • The game installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SalvageArena. Your saves live separately in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SalvageArena and updates never touch them.
  • To uninstall, delete those two folders and the launcher.
